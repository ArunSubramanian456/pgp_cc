Cheat sheets for revision

One page per topic, in course order. Each links back to its lesson.

How to revise (so it sticks) Don't just reread. Read a sheet's headings only, say out loud what's under each, then check. Do two or three sheets at a time, mixed from different parts of the course, a few days apart. Rereading feels productive but builds little; recalling first is what builds long-term memory.
